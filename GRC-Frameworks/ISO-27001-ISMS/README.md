## Understanding ISO/IEC 27001

ISO/IEC 27001 is an internationally recognised standard that defines requirements for establishing, implementing, maintaining and continually improving an Information Security Management System (ISMS).

The standard helps organisations systematically manage information security risks and protect the confidentiality, integrity and availability of information.

### Key Areas of ISO 27001

| Area | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Information Security Governance | Establish security policies, objectives and responsibilities |
| Risk Assessment | Identify and evaluate information security risks |
| Risk Treatment | Select appropriate controls to address identified risks |
| Access Control | Restrict access to authorised users |
| Asset and Data Protection | Protect sensitive information and business assets |
| Incident Management | Establish processes for managing security incidents |
| Compliance | Support applicable legal, regulatory and contractual obligations |
| Continual Improvement | Review and improve information security practices |

## Practical Training Scenario

### Marks & Spencer (M&S UK) — ISMS Policy Simulation

**Scenario:**

As part of my training, I worked with a simulated information security management scenario based on Marks & Spencer's UK operations.

The exercise focused on protecting customer and employee information, addressing cybersecurity risks and understanding how an organisation could establish security policies aligned with ISO/IEC 27001.

### 1. ISMS Scope and Objectives

The training scenario covered:

- Customer-facing applications and internal systems
- Digital infrastructure and cloud services
- Employees, contractors and third-party suppliers
- Customer and organisational information assets
- Protection of confidentiality, integrity and availability (CIA)

**Learning focus:** Understanding the importance of defining an ISMS scope, security objectives and organisational responsibilities.

### 2. Identity and Access Management

**Security controls explored:**
- Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- Least-privilege access
- Periodic user access reviews
- Restricting access to sensitive information

**Learning focus:** Understanding how identity and access management controls help reduce unauthorised access and account compromise risks.

### 3. Third-Party and Supplier Risk Management

The scenario included security requirements for suppliers and service providers.

**Key areas explored:**
- Supplier security assessments
- Security requirements before vendor onboarding
- Third-party compliance reviews
- Vendor risk documentation
- Protection of information shared with suppliers

**Learning focus:** Understanding how third-party risk management contributes to an organisation's overall information security posture.

### 4. Data Protection and Encryption

The ISMS policy exercise addressed the protection of sensitive information.

**Security controls explored:**
- Encryption of sensitive data at rest
- Encryption of data in transit
- Protection of customer personally identifiable information (PII)
- Secure handling of organisational information

**Learning focus:** Understanding how encryption and data protection controls support information confidentiality and integrity.

### 5. Security Awareness and Training

The simulated policy included employee security awareness requirements.

**Key areas explored:**
- Phishing awareness
- Social engineering risks
- Secure password practices
- Employee security responsibilities
- Regular cybersecurity awareness training

**Learning focus:** Understanding how employee awareness helps reduce human-related cybersecurity risks.

### 6. Cybersecurity Incident Response

The exercise also covered information security incident management.

**Key areas explored:**
- Documented incident response plans
- Security incident reporting
- Defined responsibilities
- Incident response testing
- Coordination between security and business teams

**Learning focus:** Understanding the role of incident management within an ISMS.

## Security Governance and Responsibilities

The simulated ISMS policy identified responsibilities for several organisational roles.

| Role | Security Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) | ISMS ownership and security governance |
| IT Security Team | Security control implementation, monitoring and audits |
| HR Department | Employee security awareness and onboarding |
| Vendor Managers | Supplier security and compliance oversight |
| Employees | Following security policies and reporting incidents |

This exercise strengthened my understanding of the importance of clearly defined security responsibilities and accountability.
