# Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM) — Practical GRC Training

## Overview

As part of my Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC) training through **Hands-on Agile Coaching**, I studied Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM) and its role in identifying, assessing and mitigating cybersecurity risks associated with external vendors, suppliers and service providers.

I used **Atlassian Confluence** to practise organising and documenting third-party security policies, risk assessment exercises and incident response procedures.

My training included simulated scenarios involving the aviation and healthcare industries, with a focus on supplier security, cloud misconfigurations, data protection, compliance obligations and risk treatment.

## Understanding Third-Party Risk Management

Third-Party Risk Management is the process of identifying, evaluating, monitoring and managing risks introduced by external organisations that access, process, store or support business information and systems.

### Key Areas of TPRM

| Area | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Vendor Identification | Identify third parties and the services they provide |
| Risk Assessment | Evaluate cybersecurity and operational risks |
| Due Diligence | Review vendor security practices and supporting evidence |
| Control Evaluation | Assess the adequacy of existing security controls |
| Risk Treatment | Recommend actions to reduce identified risks |
| Contractual Compliance | Establish security and incident reporting requirements |
| Continuous Monitoring | Review vendor security risks throughout the relationship |
| Incident Response | Coordinate the response to third-party security incidents |

## Practical Training Scenarios

### 1. Qantas — Third-Party Cybersecurity Risk Assessment

**Scenario:**

A simulated Qantas third-party risk assessment involving FlyRewards Ltd., a vendor responsible for loyalty campaign management.

The scenario involved a misconfigured Amazon S3 storage bucket that exposed customer information, including names, email addresses, frequent flyer numbers and loyalty account details.

**Key risks examined:**

- Publicly accessible cloud storage
- Exposure of personally identifiable information (PII)
- Inadequate cloud security controls
- Weak vendor security oversight
- Insufficient security monitoring
- Regulatory and reputational consequences

**Risk assessment areas:**

- Threat and vulnerability identification
- Data classification
- Inherent risk assessment
- Security control evaluation
- Residual risk assessment
- Risk treatment recommendations

**Security controls explored:**

- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- Cloud security configuration reviews
- Encryption of sensitive information
- Vulnerability management
- Incident response planning
- Vendor compliance assessments
- Centralised security logging

**Learning focus:**

Understanding how third-party cloud security weaknesses can expose organisational data and how structured vendor risk assessments help identify and address those risks.

### 2. Healthcare — Third-Party Risk Assessment

**Scenario:**

A fictional healthcare organisation, MediPlus Health Services, planned to engage HealthDocs, a SaaS provider responsible for processing patient records and supporting appointment bookings.

The training scenario involved assessing cybersecurity and compliance risks before vendor onboarding.

**Key risks examined:**

- Lack of Multi-Factor Authentication on administrative accounts
- Uncertainty regarding patient data residency
- Inadequate contractual breach notification requirements
- Missing recent penetration testing evidence
- Potential exposure of sensitive healthcare information

**Risk assessment activities explored:**

- Identifying vendor-related security risks
- Evaluating likelihood and business impact
- Assigning risk ratings
- Reviewing security control requirements
- Developing risk mitigation recommendations
- Considering regulatory and contractual obligations

**Learning focus:**

Understanding how healthcare organisations can evaluate third-party providers before onboarding and reduce risks associated with sensitive patient information.

## Risk Assessment Methodology

Through these training exercises, I explored a structured approach to third-party risk assessment.

### Step 1 — Establish Context

- Identify the vendor and business relationship.
- Determine the information and systems involved.
- Define assessment scope and compliance requirements.

### Step 2 — Identify Risks

- Identify threats and vulnerabilities.
- Review sensitive data exposure.
- Examine access control, cloud security and vendor governance weaknesses.

### Step 3 — Assess Inherent Risk

- Evaluate the likelihood of identified risks.
- Determine potential business impact.
- Assign risk ratings before considering mitigating controls.

### Step 4 — Evaluate Security Controls

- Review authentication and access controls.
- Examine encryption and data protection measures.
- Assess incident response capabilities.
- Review vulnerability management and security monitoring.

### Step 5 — Assess Residual Risk

- Consider the effectiveness of existing controls.
- Identify remaining security exposure.
- Determine whether additional mitigation is required.

### Step 6 — Recommend Risk Treatment

- Identify appropriate corrective actions.
- Recommend security improvements.
- Define responsible stakeholders and target completion dates.
- Document risk treatment recommendations.
