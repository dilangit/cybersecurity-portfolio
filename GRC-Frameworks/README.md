
## Hands-on GRC Training & Confluence Documentation

**Training Provider:** Hands-on Agile Coaching  
**Platform:** Atlassian Confluence  
**Area:** Cybersecurity Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC)

As part of my cybersecurity professional development, I undertook practical GRC training through Hands-on Agile Coaching, using Atlassian Confluence to practise organising and maintaining cybersecurity governance documentation.

My training involved working through simulated business scenarios across banking, insurance, healthcare, government, retail and aviation sectors.

### Practical Training Areas

**1. NIST Cybersecurity Framework**
- Studied the application of the NIST Cybersecurity Framework to different business environments.
- Worked through scenarios involving banking applications, healthcare records, insurance platforms and government services.
- Explored asset identification, security vulnerabilities, risk mitigation and incident response requirements.

**2. ISO/IEC 27001 — Information Security Management**
- Worked with an ISMS policy simulation based on a UK retail organisation.
- Explored information security objectives, access controls, supplier risk, encryption and incident response.
- Developed an understanding of how documented security policies support information security governance.

**3. Healthcare Security and HIPAA**
- Studied healthcare data protection and privacy policy requirements.
- Explored procedures for identifying, containing, reporting and managing breaches involving protected health information.
- Reviewed responsibilities across security, privacy, legal and compliance teams.

**4. Third-Party Risk Management**
- Worked through simulated vendor security risk assessments.
- Examined risks involving cloud misconfigurations, weak authentication, data protection and vendor incident reporting.
- Studied inherent risk, residual risk, control effectiveness and risk treatment planning.

**5. Cybersecurity Incident Response**
- Studied major cybersecurity incident response policies and procedures.
- Explored incident detection, escalation, containment, recovery, regulatory reporting and post-incident review.

### Confluence Documentation Practice

Used Atlassian Confluence as a practical learning environment to organise and document GRC-related activities, including:

- Information security policies and procedures
- Risk assessment exercises
- Third-party risk management documentation
- Incident response documentation
- Security governance and compliance learning notes

### Training Outcome

This practical training helped strengthen my understanding of how cybersecurity frameworks, risk assessments, documented controls and compliance processes support organisational security.

It complements my technical cybersecurity learning and enterprise IT support experience, while supporting my development towards cybersecurity analyst, information security risk and GRC analyst roles.

*All scenarios described are training exercises and simulations, not production assessments or professional engagements with the organisations referenced.*
